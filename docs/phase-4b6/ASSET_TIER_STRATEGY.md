# Asset Tier Strategy

## Tier A — Hero Asset

Hook, Turning and Ending use complete visual-metaphor crops: torn paper and
light, a bending road, and a figure walking into light. These carry emotional
and compositional weight.

## Tier B — Detail Asset

The toggle switch, clock, hand and paper heart are object/detail crops. They
support one concept or action without requiring a complete illustration per
segment.

## Tier C — Layout Asset

Keywords, paper ground, short labels, borders and restrained shadows are
programmatic layout elements. They do not pretend to be independent hero
artwork.

## Current fixture boundary

For deterministic Phase 4B.6 testing, all visual crops come from the approved
text-free Direction A atlas and remain asset-hash bound. This validates tiered
usage and scene behavior, not an automated asset-selection pipeline. No real
book, provider call, stock download or new commercial asset was introduced.
