# Current Video Critique

## Confirmed strengths

`PaperCollageVisualV1` has already proven the Renderer Contract, staged assets, Final Mix, sentence captions, Attempt isolation, Result/Handoff, technical QC and full-motion safe-area checks.

## Visual problems that block Phase 4C

1. The structure stays close to “central image card + bottom caption card”.
2. Programmed geometric fixtures prove plumbing, not editorial expression.
3. Paper collage is mostly communicated by beige, border and shadow; effective layers are limited.
4. Frequent content cuts reuse nearly identical composition.
5. Motion and transition are technically stable but provide little rhythm.
6. The detached caption box competes with the main visual.
7. The opening card is readable but does not provide a strong visual hook.
8. Keywords, metaphor, contrast, close-up and emotional progression are underdeveloped.

## Decision

Phase 4C is paused. No real book content is authorized until the user selects a redesigned visual direction. Phase 4B.5 therefore produces static direction boards only; it does not modify Remotion or render a full video.
