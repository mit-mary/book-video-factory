## Recommendation, not decision

For a viewpoint-driven short video, Direction C is the strongest primary recommendation because it has the clearest hook, spatial change and conceptual identity. If a mixed direction is allowed, the most promising combination is **C as the visual-metaphor system with A’s editorial typography and torn-paper treatment**.

Direction B remains the safest choice when calm reading, psychological reflection and automation reliability are more important than immediate visual impact.

The final choice belongs to the user.
